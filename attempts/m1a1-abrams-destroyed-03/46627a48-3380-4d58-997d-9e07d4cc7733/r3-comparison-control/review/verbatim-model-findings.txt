4. **The hole reads more like a hatch than in v85.** v85's local halo is gone, and the clean gray deck makes the crisp-rimmed opening look like a designed access hole.

6. **The rim and cavity geometry are unchanged:** still a straight rim with no torn metal. This was preserved as intended, and the prior v72 physical findings stand.
