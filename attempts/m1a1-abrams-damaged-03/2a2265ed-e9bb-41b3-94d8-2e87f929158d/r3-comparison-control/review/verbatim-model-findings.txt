### Strongest objections
1. **The finish only shows in one view.** It reads in `1000-top.png` but not in `1000-rear-three-quarter.png`, the main oblique view of the deck. As a fire cue, it adds almost nothing over v69.
2. **The soot looks like a smudge.** The soft, even falloff reads as an airbrushed brown smudge, not soot pattern from heat leaving a vent.
3. **The grille layout still differs from clean.** The two-bay layout doesn't match the single grille on clean v1.0, as the prior findings already noted, and v84 doesn't resolve that.
4. **The slats could still read as debris.** The brown curled slats in `1000-rear-three-quarter.png` could still pass for loose material, and the soot doesn't help them read as a warped frame.
5. **Recognition is intact.** The tank reads as an M1A1 in every view, and I have no objection to its primary forms. It reads as a lightly damaged tank only from close range.

### Recommendation: **KEEP THIS FINISH DIRECTION WITH LIMITS**
The soot is harmless and in the right place, at the level of restraint the brief asked for. Limits:
- It doesn't show fire beyond the 1000 top view.
- It must not be counted as visible at 256 or 128.
- The grille-layout question still needs a decision.

**The physical direction (the bent grille) is still useful.** The prior D03 v69 findings stand, and no approval transfers.
