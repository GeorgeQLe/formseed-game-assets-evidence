### Strongest objections
1. **The blot looks like an ink blot.** It is a rounded, lobed cloud with a uniform black core and a soft halo, and it has no directional streaking. It could also pass for spilled oil, a burnt patch or a decal-like stain rather than soot patterning.
2. **Pale specks inside the blot are ambiguous.** In `1000-top.png` at about x≈520–575, y≈150–190, they are close to the background's blue-gray. They could read as ash or as see-through holes.
3. **The blot stops beside a clean tan fender.** On the right, the black ends right next to the fender lip at about x≈625–635. This could be occlusion, but it may read as an owner cutoff.
4. **The two-bay grille still differs from clean v1.0's single grille.** This charge carries over unresolved.
5. **No damage reads anywhere else.** This is consistent with a "damaged", not "destroyed", brief. Recognition and primary silhouette are intact at every size. I make no claim about thermal or other cause.

**Grille coherence:** the soot context now makes the brown curled slats read more like a warped, scorched grate than loose debris. It is a modest gain; the framed bay still reads as one deformed grate next to an intact one.

**Does the whole model read as damaged at game sizes?** Yes in top-down views at 128, 256 and 1000, and in the oblique view at 256 and 1000. It does not read in oblique 128.

### Recommendations (informal)
- **Finish: KEEP DIRECTION WITH LIMITS.**
  - Break up the blot shape with streaking or a falloff tied to the vents.
  - Resolve the pale specks.
  - Don't count it as visible at 128 oblique.
- **Physical geometry (the source bent grille): KEEP DIRECTION WITH LIMITS.** The grille-layout decision is still open, and the v69 and v84 limits stand. No approval transfers.
