**Strongest objections**
1. **The finish reads as an airbrushed weathering gradient, not as broad irregular fire or heat (`1000-right.png`, `1000-top.png`).** It has no mottling, no burnt-through paint and no ash pattern at the creases that I can see. It's subtle, not striped.
2. **It doesn't communicate "destroyed" (all views).** The turret, gun, antennas, road wheels, tracks and paint are all intact and clean. `usmc-hansen.jpg` shows what the category looks like: a total burnout with blackened, mottled paint across the hull and turret, burnt running gear and ash on the ground. That photo is cross-period evidence for the category only, not for this event or its contours.
3. **The vane remnant sticks out past the rear plate** (`1000-right.png`, `1000-top.png`). In the side view it reads as a rake. From the top it looks detached. It needs an attachment check from an angle where the join is visible.
4. **It can't be told apart at 128**, and the breach is only a small notch at 256.

**Informal recommendation: retain for finishing, with limits.** The rear breach is a sound physical direction, and it reads as damage, not maintenance. The v68 texture is a weak first pass that doesn't read as fire. The **destroyed condition as a whole is still unfinished**: it needs a burnout treatment across the vehicle, and probably secondary consequences on the turret and running gear, before a destroyed read is plausible. That's a scope note, not an invented incident.
