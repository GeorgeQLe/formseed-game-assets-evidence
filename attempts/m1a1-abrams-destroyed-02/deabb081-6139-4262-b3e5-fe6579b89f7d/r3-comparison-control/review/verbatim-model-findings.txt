### 1000 and 128 against the same cameras
**`1000-rear-three-quarter.png` vs v78, v54 and clean:**

- **v87:** a broken pattern with irregular edges:
  - black (x≈650–700, y≈740–830 and x≈880–910, y≈690–720);
  - mid-grey and pale grey (x≈690–760, y≈705–760 and x≈840–880, y≈700–740);

- Pale tones are now present, which answers v78 objection 1 in kind.
- **The cavity** (bent vanes at x≈705–790, y≈740–805) now sits against a lighter grey area and separates from the plate again. It is clearly more legible than in v78's `1000-rear-three-quarter.png`, though slightly less than on the plain tan plate in v54.
- At 1000 it still reads as **fractured**, not as an open service door, because the vanes are splayed and irregular.
- **The risk:** soft-edged black/grey/tan blotches on a flat-shaded tank are very close to **disruptive three-tone camouflage**. Nothing is charred, blistered or exposed as metal, and there is no soot plume. Only the confinement to the rear argues against a camo reading. Hansen (`usmc-hansen.jpg`) shows real burnout can also look blotchy, but its main cues are white ash, bare light metal and debris on the ground, and v87 has none of these at comparable strength.

### Source, material, form, physical damage and recognition
- **Source and form:** The v54 cavity and vanes appear preserved. There is no new geometry, and the vane still protrudes as recorded previously.
- **Material:** The finish has improved in kind, from airbrushed plateau to broken char with pale tones, but it is ambiguous between char and camouflage.
- **Physical damage:** The cavity is readable again at 1000, marginal at 256 and lost at 128.
- **Recognition:**
  - Roughly 85–90% of the vehicle is still clean tan, and the front three views show nothing.
  - At 256 and 128 the vehicle does **not** read destroyed: it reads as a tank with a dirty or patterned rear.

### Strongest objections
1. **The destroyed reading as a whole fails** at the gameplay scales (`256-rear-three-quarter.png`, `128-rear-three-quarter.png`, `128-right.png`, `256-front-three-quarter.png`). A rear-only burn has to carry "destroyed" by itself, and it doesn't.
2. **It can be read as camouflage.** In `1000-rear-three-quarter.png` and `1000-top.png` the blotches look like a disruptive pattern.
3. **Left and right are still symmetric** (`1000-left.png` and `1000-right.png`).
4. **The side-view fades are still soft ramps** (`1000-right.png`).

**Recommendation: KEEP THIS DIRECTION WITH LIMITS.** The direction is mottled, multi-tone ash with the v54 cavity left readable.
- **Progress on v78's objections:**
