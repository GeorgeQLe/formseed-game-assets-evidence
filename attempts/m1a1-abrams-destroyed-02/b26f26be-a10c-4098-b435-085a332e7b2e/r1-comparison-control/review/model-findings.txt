## W02: destroyed 02, fractured grille (r1)

**`technicalValidation`:** passes, per your report. The audit shows Hull and the rear exhaust grilles changed, plus a new grille remnant; all 267 other parts are untouched. Every solid is clean and closed. The addendum confirms the final length of 10.1186 m sits inside the narrow 10.12 ± 0.1% bound. The camera framing is unchanged.

**1. Does it fix the old objection (clean maintenance opening vs fracture)?** Largely, yes.
- **Old v46:** a clean rectangular slot across two grille bays, with a thin wire. It read as an opened service bay.
- **v54:**
  - the lower edge is irregular, with broken louver stubs;
  - the centre bay is only partly lost;
  - a connected piece of vane and frame twists outward from the left side;
  - the right bay is intact.

  That reads as a fracture.
- **What's left:**
  - the top and side edges of the opening still follow the original rectangular bay;
  - at 256 and 128 the outward-swung piece could be mistaken for a grille door opened on its hinges. The M1 manual (p.59) shows exactly that kind of hinged, liftable door, and the source packet flags this risk itself.

**2. Support and what sits behind the opening.** Mostly credible.
- Rays through the opening hit the Hull about 0.44–0.48 m deep, so the opening is a closed recess, not see-through. The audit confirms the attachment pieces sit 9–12 mm into the Hull.
- Weak point: the fractured lower frame piece is only 0.18 mm inside the surface. That's essentially surface contact, not a real anchor; it's worth confirming in the native isolation check.
- The audit also doesn't re-list every pairwise contact, so I couldn't independently confirm the "connected" claim beyond what it lists.

**3. Does it still look like the same tank?** Yes. Front, flanks, turret and top deck match the clean model.

**4. All six 256 views, and is hiding honest?**
- **Rear three-quarter:** the disrupted bays are clear.
- **Left and right:** a small comb-like tab sticks out past the rear plate.
- **Top:** a small tab is visible at the rear edge and isn't clipped.
- **Front and front three-quarter:** unchanged, which is honest.
- **Close-up side profiles (1000):** the vanes seen edge-on read as thin comb/ladder lines, which brings back a little of the old "thin wires" impression.

**5. 128 px.** In the rear three-quarter view the grille pattern visibly breaks across two bays, so it's legible. The side and top views show only a pixel or two of protrusion, which is acceptable.

**Sources**
- **What the sources support:** the M1 1983 manual (p.59) shows the louvered, hinged grille-door anatomy, though it's the M1, not the M1A1. MCO p.23 confirms "rear grille doors" on the M1A1. Source packet v6 (from the DoD report) supports a historical case with damaged rear grille doors followed by a fire.
- **What's designed:** none of these gives a measured break, opening outline or fragment position. The fragment's pose and the 0.29 m it sticks out are design choices.

**Strongest objections**
- **Visual:** the straight top and side edges, and the comb-like look in profile.
- **Mechanical:** the near-zero anchor on the lower frame piece, and the risk of reading as an opened service door.
- **Recognition (biggest):**
  - **No "destroyed" read yet:** apart from the rear grille, the tank is pristine. As geometry alone it reads as "damaged rear grille", not destroyed.
  - **Unmet geometry target:** the destroyed-02 target in the source packet also calls for a "deformed surrounding cover". The surrounding rear plate is still straight. That's a geometry gap, not something finishing can fully cover.
  - **Overlap with D03:** the D03 target ("fractured exterior rear fitting, still attached") overlaps this. The two need checking against each other once D03 exists.
- **Material:** the dark cavity is shading, not black paint, which is fine.

**Remaining whole-condition work:**
- spread scorch and fire damage beyond the grille;
- soot on the rear deck and around the opening;
- decide whether the surrounding rear cover needs some deformation;
- review it against D03 for distinctness.

**`visualRecommendation`:** **keep the physical direction for finishing, with the limitations above.** One condition: whoever owns the brief should decide whether "deformed surrounding cover" is a binding geometry requirement. If it is, that's a follow-up geometry pass, not a rejection of this grille work.
