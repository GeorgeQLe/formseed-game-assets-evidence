### Strongest objections
1. **The burnout is not credible as a whole (core).** About 75–80% of the visible surface is still clean tan: turret, deck, glacis, gun, hatches and antennas. The answer to the brief's question is: **pristine tan vehicle plus flat black skirt plus a smeared, airbrushed patch** (`1000-right.png`, `1000-front-three-quarter.png`). There is no pale ash, no mottling and no turret involvement. With only two RightTurretCheek faces selected, turret change could never be visible.
2. **The material reads as paint.** The gradients are cut off by panel seams (`1000-right.png`, panels 3–5). This matches the predicted risk at the 0.22 hard-edge boundary.
3. **The chock/fragment ambiguity is unchanged.** The geometry is the same, so the per-wheel pads still read as chocks and the four-shoe fans still read as laid-out spares (`1000-front-three-quarter.png` x≈450–545, y≈790–845; `1000-rear-three-quarter.png` x≈815–925, y≈795–840). The overlap with D02's front shoe group also remains open.
4. **The physical precursor's escalation condition is triggered.** The v62 review required that if finishing could not make the model read as destroyed in the 3/4 views at 256, the geometry (wheel-station disruption) must be reopened. v77 does not get there.

I am not inventing any physical failure. These are statements about what is visible.
