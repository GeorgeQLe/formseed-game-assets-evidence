### Strongest objections
1. **It reads as a camouflage or dark-paint scheme, not a burnout.**
   - The map is cool neutral gray at one mid-dark value, with whitish soft streaks.
   - It has no char-black, rust-brown or bare-metal extremes. S/usmc-hansen.jpg, used only as a broad analogue, varies across every face.
   - At 128 the streaks vanish and leave "dark gray tank".
2. **The pattern looks regular.** The same slant and stroke width repeat across the top, which reads as one repeated brush. It isn't a hard tile.
3. **The localization cue was lost.** v89's hatch-concentrated soot was its strongest cue, and nothing replaces it.
4. **Clean pale parts remain:**
   - road wheels on both sides;
   - sprocket and idler hubs;
   - cream bow and headlight frames with clear blue glass;
   - tan roof sight and ammo boxes and MG pintle parts;
   - lift brackets and antenna bases.
5. **Seams and chart splits:** I saw no obvious hard UV split line at 1000. The mantlet and the right turret wedge do read as separately toned panels. The 0.14-linear hard-edge risk is not observed at these sizes, which doesn't prove it is absent.

### Physical geometry
- v94's continuous steel loop is retained and coherent: no chocks, no hover, and the front view is symmetric.
- The separate concern still stands: the loop reads as intact, functioning track.
- 96 source shoes don't establish an exact real incident.
- The bright wheels against the dark hull amplify the intact read.

**Does the whole model read as destroyed at game sizes?** No.

### Recommendations (informal)
- **Finish: KEEP DIRECTION WITH LIMITS.**
  - The shared whole-body chart carrier is right: it fixes v89's left-side, top and tan-casing gaps.
  - The map content fails as a burnout read. It needs char-black and rust value and hue extremes, soot concentrated at openings, broken streak regularity, and darkened wheels, hubs and hardware.
- **Physical geometry: KEEP DIRECTION WITH LIMITS.** This is unchanged from v94, and any requirement for visible track or running-gear damage is still open.

All prior failures and charges are preserved: D03 v69 and v84, and W01 v62, v77, v89 and v94. Nothing here is a score, a rater record, source approval or human approval.
