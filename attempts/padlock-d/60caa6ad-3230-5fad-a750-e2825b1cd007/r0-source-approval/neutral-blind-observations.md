# Neutral blind before/after observations — soft-rounded v1 vs polish p1

Date: 2026-10-06. Independent reviewer; inputs limited to the six blind sheet PNGs (hashes-of-neutral-inputs.txt). Recorded verbatim before unsealing SEALED-mapping.json.

Neutral first-impression review of padlock icons X and Y

Caveat: the 4× sheets display quite small in my viewer, so I'm confident about overall impressions but less sure of single-pixel details. Coordinates are approximate, on the 32×32 grid.

**Version X**
- **Whole object:** A padlock. The body is a wide, squat brass or bronze rounded rectangle, about x4–28, y13–29. The shackle is blue-grey steel, about x8–24, y2–14. The keyhole is a black circle with a slot, centred near x16, y18–23. Light comes from the upper left: the top-left body rim and the left shackle leg are lighter, and the right and bottom edges are darker. It reads as a solid metal lock with soft, slightly rounded volume.
1. **Readability at native size:** Reads immediately as a lock on all three backgrounds.
   - Light background: the pale grey-blue upper shackle loses some contrast, but the outline holds it.
   - Dark background: everything is clear.
   - Checkerboard: the shackle highlight competes a little with the grey squares, but nothing disappears.
2. **Left/right balance:**
   - Shackle: the right leg looks marginally heavier and darker than the left, about x20–24 against x8–12. Most of that seems to be value (shadow side), not width.
   - Body: the shoulders look close to symmetric. The right edge reads slightly heavier because of its dark rim.
3. **Stripes and bands:** There is a lighter rim band along the top of the body (y~13–14) and a darker band along the bottom (y~27–29). Both help: they sell the bevel. I see no distracting seam across the middle of the body.
4. **Shading:**
   - Light direction is consistent from the upper left.
   - The body reads as gently bevelled rather than flat.
   - The shackle has a clear lit side and shadow side and reads as a round tube.
   - Clusters look fairly clean. The arch curve has mild stair-stepping at the top corners (about x9–11 and x21–23, y3–5).
5. **Strength:** Solid, instantly readable silhouette with a convincing metal contrast between brass and steel.
   **Weakness:** The shackle arch is slightly blocky at the corners, and the light background lowers its contrast.

**Version Y**
- **Whole object:** The same padlock design and palette, with the same upper-left light. The differences are subtle. The shackle arch looks a touch rounder and more even, and the body's shading looks a little smoother across its face.
1. **Readability at native size:** At 1× it is essentially indistinguishable from X on all three backgrounds. It has the same slight shackle fade on light and checkerboard, and nothing merges away.
2. **Left/right balance:** The shackle legs look more evenly matched in visual weight than in X, though the right leg is still darker as the shadow side. The body shoulders look symmetric.
3. **Stripes and bands:** It has the same top highlight rim and bottom shadow band, and they help in the same way. I see no unwanted seam or frame line.
4. **Shading:**
   - Consistent upper-left light.
   - The body bevel reads at least as well as in X, slightly smoother.
   - The shackle reads as a rounded tube.
   - I see no obvious speckle or stray pixels, and the arch corners look marginally less jagged than in X.
5. **Strength:** Slightly cleaner, more balanced shackle curve with the same strong silhouette.
   **Weakness:** It is barely different from X at native size, and the shackle still loses contrast on light backgrounds.

**Preference:** I personally slightly prefer Version Y. Its shackle arch looks a bit rounder and its two legs better balanced in visual weight, which makes the lock feel more finished. At true 1× game size, though, I could not reliably tell the two apart. Both are strong, readable icons. If one thing is worth improving in either, it is the contrast of the shackle's upper arch against light backgrounds.
