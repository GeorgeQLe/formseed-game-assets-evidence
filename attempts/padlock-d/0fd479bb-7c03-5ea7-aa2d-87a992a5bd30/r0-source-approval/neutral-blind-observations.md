# Neutral first observations — D style studies (blind)

Date: 2026-10-05. Reviewer: independent subagent with access only to the six blind sheet PNGs (hashes in hashes-of-neutral-inputs.txt); no repository access, no study names, no disclosed hypotheses. Recorded before unsealing SEALED-mapping.json. Coordinates are the reviewer's own estimates from the 8x sheets and include a small label/margin offset.

## Study A
Brass/bronze body, blue-grey steel shackle, slight three-quarter view. Left side lighter bevelled face ~3–4 px (x5–9) with vertical highlight streak ~x7; right side darker band ~x24–27. Shackle left leg pale highlight column, right leg darker. Light from upper-left; chunky block with depth.
1. Readability: clearly a padlock at 1x on all three; on dark the right edge and bottom row lose definition; on checker the shackle mid-grey partly blends with grey squares.
2. Balance: body clearly asymmetric — left face reads wider/"fatter" than right edge; stepped bottom-left corner (~x5–9, y25–30) reads chamfered/cut away; right shackle leg looks slightly heavier/darker. Reads lopsided rather than rotated at 1x.
3. Stripes: x7 highlight streak reads as a separate stripe/seam competing with keyhole; dark right band works as side plane.
4. Shading: consistent light; bottom-left staircase jaggy; shackle highlight pixels near the arch top slightly noisy.
5. Strength: most dimensional. Weakness: lopsided silhouette, busier clusters at 1x.

## Study B
Flat front-on brass padlock, thick uniform blue-grey shackle; tiny upper-left arch highlight; small L-shaped highlight at body top-left; dark bottom row.
1. Readability: very clear on light; dark bottom edge fades on dark; on checker the flat shackle grey has the lowest contrast of the three and its outline softens.
2. Balance: essentially symmetric; thick shackle makes the arch slightly bulbous relative to the body.
3. Stripes: only the bottom shadow row, which grounds the body.
4. Shading: clean, almost no speckle; very flat; corner highlight reads as an isolated decal.
5. Strength: cleanest, most legible silhouette. Weakness: low volume; shackle lacks separation on mid-grey.

## Study C
Front-on brass padlock, thinner steel shackle with dark outer edge and light inner band along the arch top; body light top rim row, 1-px light left edge, dark right edge and bottom row. Light upper-left, consistent.
1. Readability: reads well at 1x on all backgrounds; dark shackle outer edge gives best separation on light and checker; on dark the dark outer shackle pixels and body right/bottom edges merge somewhat.
2. Balance: largely symmetric; left body edge very slightly lighter/thinner than right (barely noticeable); legs even; arch slightly flatter on top than B.
3. Stripes: light top rim reads as lid line/seam — mostly helpful, can look like a separate strip at 1x; shackle arch band gives a tubular feel.
4. Shading: coherent, clean, restrained rim lighting; flat body centre; no stray pixels.
5. Strength: balance of clarity, symmetry and a hint of form; best-separated shackle. Weakness: body fairly flat; edges fade on dark.

## General
No study has a dark outline, so all lose edge definition on the dark background. Keyholes identical and read well.

## Preference (stated as personal preference)
1. C — symmetric, clean, readable, just enough rim light.
2. B — clearest flat icon but plain; shackle washes out on grey.
3. A — richest shading, but asymmetric bevel and stepped bottom-left look lopsided and noisier at native size.
