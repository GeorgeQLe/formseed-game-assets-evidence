# Neutral blind observations — polish p1 vs p2

Date: 2026-10-06. Independent reviewer; inputs limited to six blind sheet PNGs (hashes-of-neutral-inputs.txt). Recorded verbatim before unsealing. Reviewer stated low single-pixel confidence; some leg descriptions do not match the mirrored leg pixels.

**Neutral first-impression review of padlock icons X and Y**

I looked only at the six sheets. The sheets show each 32×32 icon at only about 70 to 80 display pixels wide, so I cannot resolve single pixels with confidence. Below that level, **I can't reliably tell X and Y apart.**

**Whole object (both versions):** A closed padlock. It has a cool steel-blue rounded shackle on top (about x 9–23, y 2–13) and a warm brass or bronze rectangular body (about x 4–28, y 13–29). The body has slightly rounded corners and a dark keyhole in the centre (round head near x 16, y 18, with a tapered slot down to about y 23). The light seems to come from the upper left: the left leg of the shackle has a pale highlight, the right leg is darker, and the body has a lighter top and left rim with a darker right side and bottom. The body reads as a thick slab with bevelled edges, and the shackle reads as a round bar.

**1. Readability at native size:** Both read instantly as a padlock on light, dark and checkerboard backgrounds. The body is clearest on dark. On light, the blue-grey shackle contrasts less but still reads. On checkerboard, the shackle's mid-grey-blue partly blends into the grey checks. This is the weakest case, but the silhouette holds.

**2. Left/right balance:** The right shackle leg (about x 21–23) looks slightly heavier and darker than the left leg (about x 9–11). This comes from its shadow value, not its width. The left leg looks a touch thinner because its highlight eats into it. In Y, the top right shoulder of the loop (about x 20–22, y 3–4) looks slightly more stepped or squared than X's. I'm not certain of this. The body looks symmetric to me, with the darker right edge column (about x 26–27) making the right side feel a little heavier.

**3. Stripes, bands and frames:** The body has a darker perimeter band, strongest on the right and bottom (y 27–29), plus a lighter top rim (about y 14). Together they read as a bevel frame. This mostly helps by giving the body thickness. The darker bottom band slightly suggests a separate base plate or seam. I don't see a distinct groove where the shackle enters the body in either version.

**4. Shading:** The light direction is consistent in both. The body's clusters are clean, with flat fields and tidy edge bands. The shackle's curve is the noisiest area: the stepped shoulders at about y 3–5 show a few isolated tone pixels that look slightly jaggy at 4×. At 1× they vanish.

**5. Strengths and weaknesses:**
- **X:** Its strength is a clean, instantly readable silhouette with a solid brass volume. Its weakness is that the shackle's grey-blue sits close to mid-grey and fades on checkerboard.
- **Y:** Its strength is the same clarity, with a possibly firmer right shoulder. Its weakness is that the shoulder may look slightly more angular or stepped than a round bar should.

**Preference:** As a personal preference, I lean slightly towards **X**. Its loop top reads a little rounder and smoother to me. This preference is weak, because at the resolution I could view the two are nearly indistinguishable. I would want a per-pixel crop of rows 2–14 to confirm it.
