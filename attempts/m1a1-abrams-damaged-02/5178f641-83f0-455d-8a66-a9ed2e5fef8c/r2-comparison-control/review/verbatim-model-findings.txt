### (2) Strongest objections

- **Recognition (core):** The tank reads as **normal and intact** in five of the six 256 views and in both key 128 views. The front-wrap discontinuity only shows in `1000-front.png`, and faintly in `256-front.png`. In the main 3/4 view the fender hides it.
- **Primary form:** The positive cue is four flat, nearly aligned shoes. The audit gives z min ≈ 0 for all four, lateral offsets of about 3 cm and inter-shoe gaps of about 1.8 cm. That is not visibly "uneven". It reads as a **set-down spare segment or maintenance parts**, not a torn-off track. Nothing visually links the cue to the break, such as a dragged or hanging end or a run off the idler.
- **Mechanical support:** The moved shoes rest on the ground and none hover. Static support is fine. The issue is legibility, not physics. I'm not attaching any meaning to the ~0.00575° normal difference. The inherited tiny clearances from the clean base aren't something this candidate introduced.
- **Material:** Unchanged clean tan. That is acceptable for a damaged variant, but it gives the reader no help.

### (3) Comparison with the failed control and the source

- **Against `damaged02-candidate2-stack-final-v31/1000-right.png` and `1000-front-three-quarter.png`:** v31's deck stack read as stowage. v59 moves the same kind of tidy shoe pile from the deck to the ground ahead. The failure mode, "spare cargo", has moved rather than been resolved. Both versions also keep a full-looking loop in the side view.
- **Against the loose-tail concept v1:** The concept proposed about 12 shoes as a rearward tail in the right lane, with an unprompted recognition test at 128 right. v59 uses 4 shoes at the front and does not meet that check (`128-right.png`).
- **Against the sources:** `dvids-4423869-2018-1000w.jpg` is itself a **maintenance** scene, with a long track run laid on the ground. A neat flat segment on the ground literally matches that read, which is the problem. `hard-rock.png` shows a trackless running gear with the track stacked on the rear, and v31 already failed on that analogy. As stated, the sources support generic track disruption only, not this path or any incident.
- **Technical status:** 265 protected meshes are exact, native269 passes, the six masks pass and dimensions are 16/16. This is technical validation only, not visual clearance.

### (4) Recommendation

**REJECT THIS GEOMETRY.** It still reads as the normal intact tank plus loose maintenance or spare shoes. The break is only visible from the front at 1000 px. Keep v59 as a failed attempt next to the two earlier failures. Charge it under the producer ledger's iteration accounting; this review neither resets nor grants any attempt budget.
