## D02: damaged02-curved-final-v104 (job ae05776c…, r0, CP e1fdf2e6…)

**What I inspected**
- **v104:** all six 256 views; 1000 right, front, front three-quarter, left and rear three-quarter; 128 right and front three-quarter.
- **v99:** 1000 right, front, front three-quarter and left.
- **Clean:** 1000 right and left.

**What I observed**
- **Stairs and drawers are gone.**
  - In `1000-front` (screen-left track, x≈150–280, y≈790–948), the stepped gray slab stack from v99 is replaced by a dark vertical column. It is nearly indistinguishable from the intact track.
  - In `1000-front-three-quarter` (x≈490–550, y≈740–835), the light gray "chest of drawers" from v99 is now a dark strand that folds down under the front fender to the ground. It reads as track hanging off the front, not as cargo, stairs or a maintenance stand.
- **Side view, `1000-right` against clean and v99.**
  - The grounded run now continues past the last road wheel to x≈690. In clean it climbs from x≈620.
  - A short strand climbs toward the idler, and the chain wraps the idler and hangs down.
  - A forked, Y-shaped end piece hangs at x≈705–720, y≈670–700 and reaches the ground. v99's serrated strand standing past the hull is gone.
  - The break between the two ends is only weakly legible. There is a small irregular gap around x≈700–715, but it does not read as a clear separation.
- **Far track, `1000-left`.**
  - The far-track strand no longer sticks out past the bow. In v99 it did, at x≈210–250.
  - The far-side damage shows only as small dark pieces under the bow (x≈285–345, y≈680–700): the Y-shaped tip and a short detached grounded run. They read as a little debris near the idler.
  - They do not change the interpretation, and the near loop still reads as intact.
- **Smaller sizes.**
  - At 256 the damage shows only as a small dark dangle near the idler in `256-right` and a dark droop in `256-front-three-quarter`. `256-front`, `256-top` and `256-rear-three-quarter` look essentially clean; the rear three-quarter view shows the intact side, as expected.
  - At 128, `128-right` and `128-front-three-quarter` are effectively indistinguishable from clean.

**Strongest remaining objections**
1. **Weak legibility.** The break is subtle at 1000 and almost gone at 256 and 128. The disrupted track reads as "messy near the idler", not as a clearly thrown or broken track.
2. **Overlong tidy grounded run.** The grounded strand runs to x≈690 with neat, evenly spaced shoes. Neither end shows a convincing free, separated end. The Y-shaped tip looks like a hook or anchor more than a broken connector.
3. **Uncertain support.** The hanging upper end and the grounded end look as if they touch the ground, and I saw no obvious floating pieces. I can't confirm support at the shoe level from these views.
4. **Condition gap.** Apart from the track, the vehicle is identical to clean.

**Recommendation: KEEP the physical direction for further finishing, with limitations.** v104 clears the core v99 objections (stairs, drawers, the far strand past the bow, the light slab material) without adding new misreadings. The remaining work is legibility: a clearer gap and free end, and a less tidy grounded run. All earlier D02 no-go charges remain on record.
