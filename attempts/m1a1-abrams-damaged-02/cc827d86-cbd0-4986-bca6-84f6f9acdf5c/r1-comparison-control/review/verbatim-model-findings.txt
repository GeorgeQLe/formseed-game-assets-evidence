### Source, material, form, contact and recognition
- **Source and geometry:** The audit reports actual geometry matching the plan within 1 µm, and the protected objects and materials are exact. Neither says anything about how the track reads visually.
- **Material:** The steel/rubber split is unchanged. On upward-facing shoe faces the lighting makes the steel look light grey against the dark track, which drives the step/ladder reading in `1000-front-three-quarter.png` and `1000-front.png`.
- **Form:** The pads keep a constant pitch and parallel faces, with no twist and no hanging slack. The five grounded shoes in `1000-right.png` look as tidy as an intact run (the audit's own hold also flags this).
- **Contact:**
  - Ground-run contact looks the same as clean.
  - The hanging end in the right view sits about 10 px above the ground.
  - The bottom slab in the front view looks grounded.
  - I saw no clipping or hovering artefact that I'd call a defect.
- **Recognition:** In the hero view it does not read as cargo or as a maintenance layout, but it does read as a step. In the side view it reads as an extra strand. No view reads clearly as "track broken, loop open".

### Strongest objections
1. **The loop does not visibly open.** In `1000-right.png` and `256-right.png` the lower run plus the far-side climb look like the clean loop. The gap the geometry creates is hidden by projection and the fender.
2. **It still reads as steps or ladder.** See `1000-front-three-quarter.png`, `1000-front.png` and `256-front.png`. It is shorter than v74's ladder but the same kind of thing.
3. **The idler is not visibly exposed** in any view. In `1000-right.png` shoes still wrap the front of the idler.
4. **The grounded end is too regular.** In `1000-right.png`, x≈630–690, it looks like the run simply continues.
5. **No cue at 128** beyond what v74 already had.

**Recommendation: REJECT THIS DIRECTION.** I mean the local nine-pad idler-region revision as executed in v86.
- **Improvements over v74:**
  - a visible gap in the hero view;
  - outward fall in the front view;
  - a more chain-like drop.
- **Why it still fails:** every change sits in the region that the fender and far-side projection hide. A successor needs:
  - a visibly empty stretch of road wheels or idler on the near side in the side and three-quarter views;
  - irregular, twisted or slack shoes rather than parallel slats;
  - shoe value kept dark enough that they don't read as steel steps.

v74, v59 and all earlier charges stay as they are. v86 should be kept as an unapproved charged attempt. The full269 binding being prepared is not a validated successor, and I treated nothing in it as one.
