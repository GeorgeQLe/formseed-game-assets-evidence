**1000 and 128.** In `1000-left.png` the wound is a compact, nearly symmetric hex-shaped opening with diagonal strokes radiating from it. It sits in the middle of one skirt panel. In `1000-rear-three-quarter.png` it reads as a small diamond plate, like a vent. In `128-left.png` and `128-front-three-quarter.png` there is no cue at all.

**Comparison with the prior control.** I compared `damaged01-fracture-v52/1000-left.png`, `1000-front-three-quarter.png` and `1000-rear-three-quarter.png`. **I can't see a reliable difference from v67 in any of them.** If the new soot exists, it is below what these renders show me. I can't say whether it reads as a believable surface mark or as an airbrushed patch, because I can't see it. I see no distracting seams or lighting artifacts, and nothing floats.

**Strongest objections**
1. **The wound still looks manufactured or like a fitting (`1000-left.png`).** The tidy hex outline and the even radial strokes look like a designed port or emblem, not a local impact. The geometry didn't change, so this objection from v52 is still open.
2. **The v67 finish has no visible effect** at 1000, 256 or 128 compared with v52. As a finish revision, it shows no benefit I can see.
3. **Recognition at small sizes:** at 128 you can't tell it from clean, and at 256 it could be a decal. Occlusion on the opposite side is normal and doesn't call for enlarging the wound.

**Source context compared with designed choices.** `usmc-mco-8420-13-p326.png` supports skirt damage as a category: damaged areas sized by area, breaches, bent skirts. The hex outline, the rim, the soot pattern and the location are designed choices. There's no photo match.

**Damaged or destroyed?** It's clearly a damaged, still-operational tank, not a destroyed one.

**Informal recommendation: review with limits.** Treat it as a modest physical direction for a damaged variant. Don't present v67 as a finish improvement, because the soot isn't visible to me. The manufactured look of the wound outline is the open core objection.
